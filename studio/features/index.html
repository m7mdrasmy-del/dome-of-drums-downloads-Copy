01 · SITE FIT

Ground Leveling & Base Alignment

Adjust and level the remaining base nodes, align the structure to the ground plane, and complete base-ring gaps where required. These tools help reconcile the geometric shell with its practical ground-contact conditions.

CAD & 3D Printing Export

Prepare individual design parts for downstream fabrication workflows, including AutoCAD-compatible DXF geometry and STL files for parts intended for 3D printing, such as hubs, connection components and suitable custom pieces.
