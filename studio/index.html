CENTERLINES → PHYSICAL TIMBER → CONNECTION LOGIC → FABRICATION

A centerline can describe a shape. It cannot tell you how timber actually occupies space, how joints compete at a node, or whether a cut is practical. Dome of Drums is being built around the physical consequences of the geometry, not around a prettier line drawing.
